Overview

Not Just a Chatbot.
A Legal Intelligence Engine.

IP-SAKTI Sahayak is a Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipeline engineered specifically for Indian Intellectual Property law. It does not guess. It retrieves verified statutes, reranks them with 9 legal signals, grounds every answer in evidence, validates citations, and abstains when the corpus is silent.

Pipeline

The Path from
Question → Law → Answer

Every query travels through 7 deterministic stages before an answer reaches the user.

01

Query Analysis

Domain detection, intent, legal identifiers, jurisdiction

02

Hybrid Retrieval

Vector (0.55) + BM25 (0.35) + Metadata (0.10)

03

Legal Rerank

9 weighted signals — document hints, section matches, semantic overlap

04

Grounded Gen

LLM or deterministic extractive fallback

05

Citation Check

Every citation validated against evidence chunks

06

Confidence

6-factor formula — evidence count, citation density, reranker score

07

Abstention

Refuses to answer when evidence is insufficient

Capabilities

Built for

Intelligent Routing

The backend does not blindly call RAG. It classifies every query: RAG for verified law, general for broad questions, formulation for product readiness.

  • General Questions
  • Formulation Readiness
  • IP-SAKTI RAG

Voice Intelligence

Speak your query in any language. The system translates via Gemini, routes intelligently, and responds with both voice and text.

  • Speech-to-Text
  • Text-to-Speech
  • Voice Chat Overlay

Legal Guardrails

The system blocks adversarial prompts, quarantines invalid sources (Ayurveda Aahara 2022 regulation), and refuses to invent citations.

  • Exfiltration Block
  • Quarantine Checks
  • Citation Integrity 1.0

Multilingual Legal Intelligence

Queries in Hindi, English, or mixed language are translated, analyzed, and answered with citations to the verified Indian and international corpus.

  • Gemini Translation
  • Domain Routing
  • 34 Language Support
